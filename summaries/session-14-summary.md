# Session 14 Summary

## Recap
- The party was held in a Waterdeep jailhouse, mostly disarmed.
- Lluth attuned to the [[Stone of Golorr]] and made first contact with its dominant, arrogant voice.
- A figure resembling [[Eustace Staget]] killed two guards, freed the party, and urged them to flee.
- The party suspected this was an impostor due to odd behavior.
- A Darkness spell was cast as more guards (including veterans) arrived.
- The impostor loudly accused the party of killing the guards and escaping.

## Cliffhanger
- The party is surrounded by guards, framed for murder, and must decide how to respond.
