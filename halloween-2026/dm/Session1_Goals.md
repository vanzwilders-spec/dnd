# Session 1, "The Inheritance": Goals and Plan

*DM only. Gothic haunted house, Ravensmoor Manor. Level 3 to 4. Despair flavor: Isolation. Date: Friday, October 16, 2026.*

**Status:** goals and structure are a draft for your approval. Stat blocks, maps and the run sheet come after you approve this file.

## 1. Goals

1. **Make isolation felt.** The house separates the party at least once. At least one puzzle can only be solved by people in different rooms working together. [NEW]
2. **Land the premise.** Six strangers who were *found*. Mordax appears twice (the dining room mirror, the cellar pantry) and never fights. [ORIGINAL]
3. **Reward cleverness over HP.** Harwick is beaten by the fireplaces and the Furnace Room, not by out-damaging him. [ORIGINAL]
4. **Give each of the six a moment.** See the spotlight table in `Party_Roster.md`. [NEW]
5. **Plant what pays off later.** True Name Fragment #1 (Study), Luna's secret, Ashford's deal, and the first chance to earn a bond. [ORIGINAL]
6. **End at dawn, at level 4.** Stage Fill adjusted, one hook pointing at Ashvale. [ORIGINAL]

## 2. How we'll know it worked

- Every character had at least one beat where they mattered.
- The party was split at least once, and recovered by cooperating.
- Mordax was seen twice, and nobody got to hit him.
- Stage Fill is recorded and bonds earned are marked.
- Nobody left feeling the session was only combat.

## 3. Setup facts

- **Party:** 6 players, level 3. Five characters known, plus a barbarian (see `Party_Roster.md`).
- **Stage Fill starts at 3** (see `Mechanics_Fixes.md`). It carries through all four sessions.
- **If a player is absent:** use the Intermission rule in `Absence_Protocol.md`.
- **Session 0:** about 20 minutes inside the three hours (Lines & Veils, X-card, handing out the guide). [NEW, default, not yet confirmed]

## 4. Pacing for three hours with six players [CHANGED]

| Block | Minutes | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Session 0 | 20 | Safety tools, Lines & Veils, questions |
| Act 1 | 25 | Wake in the dining room mid-sentence. Ashford. Storm. Lights out. |
| Act 2 | 50 | Explore. The Collector, the Hounds, Luna, the Twins. Discover Harwick. |
| Act 3 | 40 | The cellar. Mordax in the pantry. Ashford's secret nearly out. |
| Climax | 45 | Harwick goes active. Fireplaces and the Furnace Room. Dawn. |
| **Total** | **180** | Original was 30 / 60 / 45 / 45 with no Session 0 |

Six players slow turns down, so the plan is **about two real fights** (the Collector as a short skirmish, Harwick as the climax) and the Hounds as a stealth-and-silence puzzle.
