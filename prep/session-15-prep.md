# Session 15 Prep

## Opener
- Scene resumes in the jailhouse: darkness, chaos, guards shouting, impostor present.

## Key Choices
1. Attempt to prove innocence (parley with guards, DC 18 Persuasion/Insight)
2. Flee (risk escalation, chase mechanics, DC 16 Stealth/Athletics)
3. Subdue or expose the impostor (combat/social, DC 17 Investigation/Insight)

## Encounter Beats
- Social: Guards demand surrender; impostor stokes panic.
- Combat: If violence erupts, guards act by Code Legal (nonlethal force preferred, but deadly if threatened).
- Investigation: Spotting clues about impostor’s identity (illusion, disguise, or magic traces).

## Scene Ender
- Cliffhanger: The party’s choice triggers either arrest, escape, or a dramatic reveal.

## Improv Prompts
- "Drop your weapons!"
- The smell of ozone and blood in the air
- A veteran’s voice: “That’s not the Captain!”
- The Stone’s voice in Lluth’s mind: “You are nothing without me.”
- The sound of distant bells—reinforcements?
