Session 1 Handouts: "The Inheritance"

Player-facing. Each invitation is written in the same unfamiliar hand. Send each player only their own card (and the will and guest list when the table sees them).

The invitation (one per character)

Hetfield

Mr. J. Hetfield,

You are cordially invited to attend the reading of the will of the late Lord Octavian Ravensmoor, this night, at Ravensmoor Manor. The house is very much in need of entertainment. Bring your voice. Something of yours has been kept warm by the fire.

Dinner will be served.

Butch

Mr. Butch,

You are cordially invited to attend the reading of the will of the late Lord Octavian Ravensmoor, this night, at Ravensmoor Manor. The house is full of shiny things, and the family has been told you appreciate them.

Miss Lyssandria Elmwood,

The will (read aloud by Ashford)

The Last Will and Testament of Lord Octavian Ravensmoor

I, Octavian Ravensmoor, being of unsound mind and sound appetite, declare this to be my last will.

First. The estate of Ravensmoor, with all its rooms, its hearths, its cellar and its company, passes to the one who endures the night.

Second. No guest shall leave before dawn. The bridge, the road and the weather shall be consulted.

Third. The fires of this house are to be kept. Let no hearth go cold while a guest remains in want of warmth.

Fourth. The staff are to be treated with every courtesy. They have been with me a very long time.

Fifth. Dinner is to be served.

Signed in my own hand, and a few others.
