Rulings to remember

  • Only real hearth fire stops Harwick; magic light does not.
  • Silence covers part of the Servant's Passage, not all of it.
  • Faerie Fire outlines the Hounds but does not make them solid.
  • Milo (bound to Hetfield's sword; near a lit hearth or when the sword is drawn): Hounds hesitate, T6 solved if she walks the keys, Release has advantage if she flops at Harwick's feet.
  • Nothing kills outright. A hazard drop to 0 HP means the Twins pull the character through a passage; they wake in another room at 1 HP.
  • One short rest, at the refuge hearth only.
  • Tasha's is accepted as-is. No homebrew for the players.
⚭ ⚭ ⚭

If it stalls

  • Table stuck: Luna will answer a question about the manor if she is working.
  • Table split: use the Twins' passages to move a character.
  • Table too safe: pick one hazard and let its tell be ignored once.
  • Table in trouble: the McTavern hearth is always a refuge; use Ashford's Bardic Inspiration as a lifeline.
  • Short on time: skip the Flues and let Fire come from hand-relighting.
