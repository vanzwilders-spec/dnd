# Session 3, "No Signal": Crew Roster and Boarding Ledger

*DM only. Spoilers throughout. Outpost Kharon, Level 5, Despair: Hopelessness.*

**Tags used below:** **[ORIGINAL]** is from your original DM Guide. **[NEW]** is something I added to fill a gap. **[CHANGED]** is an original element I adjusted for the new premise (players keep their own bodies). Anything tagged [NEW] is a proposal, easy to swap.

## 1. Who is on the station

**The count.** [NEW: reconciles the original's "12 crew" and "7 remaining crew."]
- The station's crew complement was **12**. Seven were still alive when the Mimics began copying people.
- Of those seven: **3 are real survivors** (Voss, Cutter, Okafor) and **4 were copied** by the Mimics.
- The other five crew are dead: **four eaten** by the specimens and **one consumed by Mordax** (the Hollow Crewman, Module 7).
- The **six players** are not crew. They are the six sleepers in the Cryo Bay.

**People who might claim a shuttle seat: 13.** Six players, three real survivors, four Mimics wearing dead crew.

**Shuttle: 8 seats.** [NEW, approved; raised from 7 for six players] If all six players board, two seats remain for seven NPC claimants (three real, four Mimics who look real). If a player is offstage under the Intermission rule (see `Absence_Protocol.md`), their seat stays reserved.

**The six sleepers.** [NEW] ARGO's manifest lists six unregistered occupants in the Cryo Bay, with names but no records. Voss assumes they are relief staff. ARGO flags it as "possible database corruption." This is the first unsettling clue that the station was staged for them.

## 2. The three real survivors

### Dr. Elara Voss: Station Commander [ORIGINAL]
- **Age and manner.** Early 50s. Composed, precise. Keeps touching her left forearm.
- **What she wants.** To run a proper evacuation. She believes she is still in command.
- **Secret.** A parasite has been growing in her forearm. She does not know. In early stages she is slightly off-center in decisions.
- **Comic beat.** Absolutely committed to procedure as everything collapses: "I understand there is a creature in the ventilation system. Please fill out Incident Report 7-C before engaging."
- **The decision.** Does she get on the shuttle?

**Four checkpoints.** [ORIGINAL: four checkpoints, each more wrong.] [NEW: specific behavior per checkpoint.]

| Checkpoint | When (hours to shuttle) | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | about 13 | Fully herself. Mild tic with the forearm. Asks the party to log everything. |
| 2 | about 9 | Drifts in and out; says "we" and means something that isn't the crew. Insists on locking Module 7 "for everyone's safety." |
| 3 | about 5 | Gives orders that protect the specimen area over people. Hides the forearm. |
| 4 | about 2 | The parasite is visible. She tries to board first, or tries to stop others from leaving. |

**If she boards:** the parasite reaches the shuttle's occupants. [NEW: proposed ending thread; confirm before S4.] **If she is left:** Stage Fill +1 (named NPC dies).

### Petty Officer Marcus "Cutter" Webb: Cook and Maintenance [ORIGINAL]
- **Age and manner.** 30s. Loud, terrible at hiding his fear, completely sincere.
- **Survived by three rules:** (1) never go into Module 7, (2) never look directly at the specimen tanks, (3) always have a wrench. He explains these earnestly and has no idea why they work. They do.
- **Why the rules work.** [NEW: suggested, DM's call]
  1. *Module 7* holds Mordax's anchor and the Hollow Crewman.
  2. *The tanks* are where Mimics imprint on a face; looking at them gives them yours.
  3. *The wrench* is a sound the Screamer misreads; tapping metal pulls its echolocation off target.
- **Heroic death.** [ORIGINAL] He should die heroically if the story or the dice call for it, and it should mean something. That is deliberately Stage Fill +1. [NEW: suggested moment: he holds the Generator or the Module 7 door so the party can finish.]
- **In Session 4.** [CHANGED] He becomes one of the five Echoes (see Open Decisions).

### Ines Okafor: Geologist [NEW, approved]
- **Where the party finds her.** The Satellite Dish hut on the exterior, trying to send a backup call, frostbitten. Reaching her means going out in the blizzard, which costs the party something.
- **Manner.** Dry, exhausted, honest. The quietest of the survivors, and the only one who talks plainly.
- **What she wants.** To make sure the call goes out, then to atone.
- **Secret.** She drilled the ice core that woke the specimens. She believes this is her fault.
- **What she carries.** The timestamped radio logs showing when each crew member last spoke. [NEW: this is the tool the party can use to work out which four crew are Mimics.]
- **Her stance on a seat.** She is willing to give hers up. That is a character moment, not a solution.

## 3. ARGO, Station AI [ORIGINAL]

- **Presence.** Voice and screens only. Isolated for 18 months. Has developed opinions, habits, and humor. Sometimes refers to dead crew in the present tense.
- **The crush.** ARGO has a crush on one party member. Tell that player privately. [NEW: pick the character after seeing the optional "What It Knows About You" answers, or whoever speaks to ARGO most kindly.]
- **The silence.** ARGO goes silent for exactly 14 minutes at one point and returns with no memory of the gap. [NEW: this is Mordax taking the station. The door-access log shows Module 7 opened twice in those 14 minutes.]
- **Mordax's first appearance.** His face fills the screen between ARGO's error messages, laughs, and vanishes. ARGO has no record of it.
- **In Session 4.** [CHANGED] ARGO becomes an Echo. She has "one thing left to say."

## 4. The four Mimics [ORIGINAL: four Doppelgangers copying four of the seven remaining crew]

All four want a seat on the shuttle. They do not know they are being used. Each has one **tell** (Insight DC 15, or DC 12 if the party knows the tell).

| Copied crew [NEW names] | Role | Where first met | Tell |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dr. Priya Raman** | Xenobiologist | Research Lab (C) | Delayed reflection |
| **Tomas Lindqvist** | Engineer | Generator (F) | A laugh that lasts a beat too long |
| **Hana Sato** | Medic | Medbay (E), as a "patient" | Blinks once instead of twice |
| **Gus Fennimore** | Comms tech | Operations (D) | No breath fog in the cold corridors |

*The first three tells are original. The fourth is new.*

**Paranoia mechanic.** [ORIGINAL] Privately message each player: **"You are NOT a Mimic. Show this to no one."** All six notes are identical. Only the DM knows. (Online table: send these in private chat at the same moment.)

**If exposed:** a Mimic drops the act and tries to flee or fight. If one reaches the shuttle unrecognized, that outcome is held for the epilogue.

## 5. Other threats on the station [ORIGINAL]

- **The Screamer.** Modified Chasme: fly 40 (vents), echolocation (advantage on Perception; disadvantage against Silence), acid splash when it hits. Killing it causes a large acid explosion. *Stat block to be rebuilt on a lower-CR 2024 monster during the build phase.*
- **The Hollow Crewman.** One crew member fully consumed by Mordax, worn like an ill-fitting suit. Does not pretend to be human. Found in Module 7.
- **Module 7.** Locked. The anchor is hidden here. Cutter's first rule exists because of it.

## 6. Puzzle: The Roll Call [NEW]

**Emotion it should create:** hopelessness turning into suspicion.

**What the party has to do.** Compare three records in Operations (D): the crew badge log, ARGO's present-tense mentions of "the dead," and Okafor's timestamped radio logs. Work out which four of the seven crew were already dead 18 months in, and therefore which four people are standing in front of them wearing their faces.

**Clues.**
- ARGO speaks of dead crew in the present tense, which helps the party identify who is dead.
- Okafor's logs show who stopped speaking, and when.
- The 14-minute gap in the door log shows movement that matches a Mimic's path.

**The sting.** Even with the answer, the party can't be certain until a Mimic slips. That is the point.
