# The Intermission: A Themed, Reusable Rule for an Absent Player

*DM only. One framework, four themed versions. When a player can't attend, their character leaves the scene in a way that belongs to the nightmare you're running, and returns in a way that belongs to it too.*

**Design target.** Up to **2 characters offstage at once** (at least 4 of the 6 at the table). Confirmed Oct 12: the limit is **per session**, not across the campaign. Absences happen, and player experience comes first.

**Tags:** **[NEW]** means I designed it. Everything here is a proposal you can edit.

---

## 1. The idea

Mordax is a director staging a show. When a player is missing, **Mordax calls an Intermission** on their character, and the scene bends around it. The character is **safe**, unreachable, and keeps their place in the story. The exit and return are written in each session's genre and despair flavor, so an absence feels like part of the horror and never like a hole in it.

Every session fills the same five slots, so you can build a new version for any scenario in a few minutes (template in section 5).

| Slot | What it is |
|---|---|
| **The Place** | Where the character is, in the fiction |
| **The Marker** | A visible thing left behind in the scene |
| **The Safe Tell** | A small, clear sign the character is fine, so the party doesn't spend the session grieving |
| **The Exit** | A short read-aloud |
| **The Return** | A short read-aloud, plus the cue card |

---

## 2. Rules (the same in every session)

**While offstage**
- The character **cannot be targeted, harmed, charmed, or affected** by anything. No turns, no initiative slot.
- The DM **does not play them**. No NPC stand-in, no hidden actions.
- **Stage Fill does not change** because of an Intermission, up or down.
- Resources freeze: HP, spell slots, hit dice, and class features stay as they were.
- **They still level up** at the end of the session (milestone) and choose their features before they return.
- **Their shuttle seat (Session 3) stays reserved.**
- Existing bonds stay. A new bond needs both players at the table.
- **Declarations (Session 4):** a present player may name an offstage character as the subject of a Declaration, and it counts. Offstage characters are not required to make one.
- **Design rule for every session:** no encounter or puzzle may require one specific class.

**Late arrivals and early exits (online tables)** [NEW]
Use the **one-line version** in each session script below. Return at the next scene break.

## 3. The four themed versions

### Session 1, Ravensmoor Manor: "The House Keeps Them" (Isolation)

| Slot | Version |
|---|---|
| **The Place** | Inside the Portrait Gallery. The house hangs them. |
| **The Marker** | A new portrait appears in the Portrait Gallery: the character, in the last pose they held, eyes open. The Gallery already "tracks the party" in the original map notes; the new portrait watches along with the rest. |
| **The Safe Tell** | The painted chest rises and falls. The frame is warm to touch, and a faint breath-fog clouds the canvas. |
| **The Exit** | *A draft crosses the room and every candle leans toward [Name]. Their reflection in the dark window stops moving a heartbeat before they do. Then they are gone, mid-step, the floorboards still settling. Somewhere on the upper floor, a single nail is tapped into plaster.* |
| **The Return** | *They step out of the nearest picture frame, smelling of linseed oil and old varnish, brushing dust from their shoulders. The portrait they left behind is blank canvas.* |
| **Cue card voice** | A house-servant's log, in neat copperplate: terse, polite, and slightly wrong. *"Staff note: the east passage has been unkind to the guests."* |
| **One-line version** | *"The house takes a breath, and [Name] is no longer in the room. Upstairs, another frame is hung."* |

*The isolation lands because the party notices the empty space and then finds the portrait: still near, still unreachable.*

### Session 2, Ashvale: "The Village Tucks Them In" (Paranoia)

| Slot | Version |
|---|---|
| **The Place** | Beneath the village, in the Root Mother's keeping |
| **The Marker** | A corn-husk doll propped where the character stood, wearing a scrap of their clothing. Villagers cheerfully refuse to notice it. |
| **The Safe Tell** | The husk doll is warm, and a green shoot grows from it. Wildflowers all around the village lean toward the Well, which is the Root Mother's way of saying "this one is mine to protect." |
| **The Exit** | *Elder Bramwick is mid-laugh when the corn behind [Name] rustles though there is no wind. A hundred husks whisper together, kindly. A hand of roots, gentle as a nurse's, closes around their ankle, and the earth takes them down like a sigh. Where they stood, a small straw figure sits as if it had always been there.* |
| **The Return** | *The Well's bucket creaks. Up the rope comes [Name], blinking, dusted in petals, smelling of rain. Every villager within sight claps once, together, and stops.* |
| **Cue card voice** | The Root Mother: petals and root-marks spelling a few words, never a full sentence. *"The fiddle fears the water."* |
| **One-line version** | *"The corn sighs. A straw figure sits where [Name] was, warm to the touch."* |

*The paranoia is in how the villagers treat the doll. If the party suspects the village, the doll's warmth and the flowers are the rebuttal. If they trust the village, the villagers' too-cheerful reaction is the doubt.*

### Session 3, Outpost Kharon: "Safety Protocol 12" (Hopelessness)

| Slot | Version |
|---|---|
| **The Place** | An emergency stasis pod in the Cryo Bay |
| **The Marker** | A frosted pod with the character's name stenciled on it. Their vitals scroll on ARGO's screens. |
| **The Safe Tell** | ARGO reports vitals as "calm, stable, regrettably well-rested." Frost forms a perfect snowflake on the glass. |
| **The Exit** | *Alarms stutter. ARGO's voice, very gently: "I'm sorry, [Name]. Safety Protocol 12." A medical gurney drone chirps an apology, scoops them up, and rolls away down the corridor. Their hand is still reaching for something that was never there.* |
| **The Return** | *The pod chimes. ARGO announces, "Thaw complete." [Name] coughs ice crystals, and their breath fogs for the first time in hours. They are alive and cold, and they have been asleep again.* |
| **Cue card voice** | ARGO's medical note, formal with a little too much warmth. *"Observed during stasis: a corridor light flickered twice, in sequence."* |
| **One-line version** | *"ARGO: 'Per Protocol 12, [Name] has been preserved for their own safety.'"* |

**Session 3 special rule.** A returning character is **never a Mimic.** ARGO's cryo log vouches for them. Don't let an absence turn into suspicion against a real player.

*Hopelessness: someone going back under, again, while everyone else keeps running out of time.*

### Session 4, The Grand Theater: "Into the Wings" (Surrender, or something else)

| Slot | Version |
|---|---|
| **The Place** | The Wings, which are literal. They watch from the dark. |
| **The Marker** | A small pool of light left on the stage where the character stood, like an ember. |
| **The Safe Tell** | The ember burns steadily. **Mordax cannot see into the Wings**, and he never thinks to look. |
| **The Exit** | *A rope sings in the rigging. The spotlight on [Name] narrows to a coin of light, then a pin, then a glow like a held match. A gloved hand, gentle, guides them into the dark. Mordax tilts his head, only slightly. "Understudy," he murmurs, as if remembering. He does not look for them.* |
| **The Return** | *[Name] walks out of the Wings into the light of their own accord. The hollow audience turns its heads in perfect unison. Mordax's smile falters for the first time.* |
| **Cue card voice** | An Echo's shaky, kind handwriting. Pick the Echo closest to that character's story. *"He cannot read the last page of his own script."* |
| **One-line version** | *"A pool of light stays on the stage where [Name] stood. They have gone into the Wings."* |

*Surrender is the theme. A character who comes back out of the dark of their own accord is a small act of defiance, and the party gets to see it happen.*

## 4. Returning: Curtain Call (all sessions)

1. **Entrance.** Use that session's Return. It happens at a quiet, safe moment, never mid-fight.
2. **The Cue Card.** A private note (chat) holding **one thing the character saw while offstage**: a minor clue the party hasn't found, never the solution to a puzzle. Include their "What It Knows About You" hook if they gave one and it was due.
3. **No bonus and no penalty.** No Inspiration, no extra loot.

## 6. Table handling

- **Token:** remove the character's token from the scene (or park it off the map), and place the Marker as an image or handout on the scene.
- **Chat:** send the cue card privately when they return. For a mid-session arrival, whisper it at the next scene break.
