Session 1 Hazard Key: "the house keeps count"

DM only. Pairs with the map PNGs in maps/ (the _DM maps show these markers; the McTavern uses an overlay). 1 square = 5 ft. House floors: 32 px per square, 40 x 26 squares each.

How strikes work [DECIDED, Oct 12]

  • Per hazard, shared by the whole party (not per player). Each hazard token carries three pips.
  • A mistake is a failed check or save at the hazard, or ignoring its tell. Add one pip.
  • Strike 1: a warning. Mild cost (at most 1d4, or a noise). Always give a tell first (a creak, draft, smell, or glint) so careful players can avoid it.
  • Strike 2: a real cost. 1d6 to 2d6, or noise that pulls the Revenant Hounds, or a split in the party.
  • Strike 3: the house closes. A lasting consequence (a route collapses, a hearth gutters early, items go to the Collector's shelf, the party is Chilled). No single trigger ever deals more than 2d6, and nothing here kills outright. A character at 0 HP from a hazard is pulled through a passage by the Twins and wakes in another room at 1 HP.
  • Reset. A hazard is solved when the party disarms it, avoids it with a clever plan, or uses it against the house. Remove its pips.
  • Hearth light matters: in an unlit room, creatures without darkvision have disadvantage on checks to spot a tell. Candles and lanterns count as dim light for tells.
  • Save DCs stay at 11 to 13, the same range as the stat blocks.

Notes

  • Set pieces. The chandelier (G2) and the catwalk (C4) are the two set pieces. Both reward bold, clever play (Sassy's swing; a rope across the pit) and punish repeated carelessness.
  • Night Clock. Strike 3 at G2 gutters the Entry Hearth early. That is in addition to the clock, never replaces it.
  • Fairness check. Every Strike 3 gives the party a way forward: nothing locks them out of Harwick's endings, and the Dining Room stays safe.
  • Open for you: confirm the three-strike costs feel right (especially G2's hearth gutter and C4's boiler pit).
