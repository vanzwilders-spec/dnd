## Sheet details: "J" Hetfield [from the D&D Beyond PDF, Oct 12]

A 2024-rules build: Bard 3, Human, Entertainer background. HP 25, AC 14 (studded leather), Initiative +4 (Alert), speed 30. STR 8, DEX 14, CON 15, WIS 10, CHA 18. **No darkvision** (Passive Perception 11).

- **Skills:** Expertise in Deception +8 and Persuasion +8; Performance +6, Intimidation +5, Stealth +4. Silver Tongue: a d20 of 9 or lower counts as 10 on Deception and Persuasion. Unsettling Words (Eloquence).
- **Feats:** Alert, Musician (Encouraging Song gives Heroic Inspiration to 2 allies after a rest).
- **Spells:** Spell DC 14, attack +6. Cantrips: Vicious Mockery, Dancing Lights. 1st (4 slots): Silvery Barbs, Dissonant Whispers, Faerie Fire, Healing Word. 2nd (2 slots): Hold Person, Suggestion.
- **Gear:** six daggers, shortbow, shortsword, a bandore, 25 gp.

**What it means for Session 1.** He can talk anyone into anything, so Ashford's confession and Harwick's Release are his. Hold Person only works on humanoids, so it won't stop Harwick. Suggestion is a charm effect and a Specter is immune to charm, so Release has to be earned by roleplay, not a spell. Faerie Fire outlines the Hounds. Healing Word is the party's only remote heal I can see. Candlelight matters: he needs it to see.

## Sheet details: Butch [from the D&D Beyond PDF, Oct 12]

Rogue 3 (Assassin), Goblin, Criminal background, 2024 rules. HP 21, AC 15, Initiative +5 with Advantage (Alert + Assassinate), speed 30, Small. DEX 16, CON 13, CHA 14, STR 8. **Darkvision 60 ft.** **Alignment: Lawful Evil** (the evil character). Traits on the sheet: skeptical, anti-social, likes shiny things, "murder hobo." Ideal: Equal Rights. Flaw: Alcoholism.

- **Skills:** Expertise in Stealth +7 and Sleight of Hand +7; Perception +3 (passive 13), Intimidation +4, Deception +4, Persuasion +4. Thieves' Cant; speaks Goblin and Undercommon.
- **Attacks:** hand crossbow +5 (1d6+3, Vex), two daggers +5 (1d4+3, Nick), Sneak Attack 2d6, Assassinate (+3 in the first round vs. creatures that haven't acted), Fury of the Small (+2 vs. larger, 2/long rest). Nimble Escape and Cunning Action: Hide/Disengage/Dash as bonus actions.
- **Gear that matters:** hooded lantern, 10 candles, 7 flasks of oil, tinderbox, 1,000 ball bearings, a bell, rope, crowbar, disguise kit, poisoner's kit, thieves' tools, 7 gp. Fey Ancestry: advantage on saves vs. charm.

**What it means.** He is the only one who sees in the dark. He is your Hounds specialist, but he also carries the **bell** (a sound lure: ring it in a far room to pull the Hounds away) and **ball bearings** (a noisy floor trap, or a quiet one if poured slowly). Oil and a tinderbox can **relight hearths**, so the Night Clock is a puzzle for him too. As the Lawful Evil character, he is the natural target for Ashford's private bargain, and the shiny-things trait fits the Collector's Trophy Room. Assassinate makes him dangerous in a first-round ambush, but not on a Specter that has already acted.

## Sheet details: Lyssandria "Sassy" Elmwood [from the D&D Beyond PDF, Oct 12]

Rogue 3 (Swashbuckler, SCAG), High Elf, Noble background, 2024 species rules. HP 24, AC 14, speed 30. **Alignment: Chaotic Good.** DEX 15, INT 13 (high elf), CHA 14. **Darkvision 60 ft.** Passive Perception 16 (Keen Senses: Perception +6, Expertise), Persuasion +6 (Expertise), Insight +4, Deception +4. Feat: Skilled. Languages: Common, Dwarvish, Elvish, Halfling, Thieves' Cant. Backstory on the sheet: youngest daughter of a disgraced noble family, trained as a leatherworker, fencing for notoriety, "happy anywhere as long as I am living on my own terms."

- **Attacks:** rapier +4 (1d8+2, Vex), dagger +4 (Nick), Booming Blade cantrip (1d8 thunder if the target moves), Sneak Attack 2d6. Fancy Footwork (no opportunity attacks from creatures she hits in melee) and Rakish Audacity (Sneak Attack without advantage when she is alone with a target in melee).
- **Spells:** Booming Blade, Detect Magic (1/long rest).
- **Gear:** 10 torches, 2 flasks of oil, tinderbox, rope, crowbar, thieves' tools, cook's utensils, leatherworker's tools, 34 gp. Trance: 4-hour long rest, immune to magical sleep. Fey Ancestry: advantage vs. charm.

**What it means.** Her duel with the Collector is built in: Fancy Footwork lets her dance around the armor, and the armor's Collect trait only bites on repeated hits. **Detect Magic** reveals the wrong-shelved book in the Study and the house's enchantments. Ten torches and oil mean she is the second hearth-relighter. Perception 16 makes her the one who hears the Twins first. Her Persuasion expertise pairs with Hetfield's for Release or Ashford. Trance does not matter in a one-night session.

Prepared options include Faerie Fire, Grease, Snare, Tasha's Caustic Brew, Catapult, Absorb Elements, False Life, Detect Magic, Identify, Alarm, Sanctuary, Disguise Self, Longstrider, Jump, Feather Fall, Expeditious Retreat, Purify Food and Drink, Cure Wounds.

- **Gear:** 10 torches, 2 oil, tinderbox, 20 caltrops, rope, crowbar, tinker's and thieves' tools, 48 gp.

**What it means.** The flamethrower is the hearth-relighter: a bonus action and a 15-ft cone sets a cold hearth burning, so the Night Clock is a puzzle he can feed (answers the open question: yes, relighting is one action, but the cannon expires after an in-game hour). **Goggles of Night** are a ready answer to the darkness: he can make a pair and hand them to Hetfield or Kurield. Grease and Tasha's Caustic Brew suit the Collector. AC 18 and 30 HP make him the sturdiest of the five casters, so Harwick's drag hits him less often. Resilient to poison, not to Life Drain.

## What the party looks like

- **Five characters use a d8 hit die** and Wolfe uses a d12. HP: Hetfield 25, Butch 21, Sassy 24, Kurield 24, Rivet 30, Wolfe 42. Wolfe is the only real frontliner.
- **Strong at skills and talking:** two Rogues, a Bard, and a Cleric with lore expertise.

- **Thin on durability** apart from Wolfe and Rivet (AC 18). Only Butch and Sassy have darkvision; Rivet can make Goggles of Night. Grapple-and-drag enemies (the Collector, Harwick) will hurt more than raw damage.
