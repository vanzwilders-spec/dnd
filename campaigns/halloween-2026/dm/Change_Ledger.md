# The Reveler's Stage: Change Ledger

Maps every section of the two original HTML guides to what happens in the redraft.

> **Superseded where it conflicts by `Decisions_Log.md`.** In particular: the table is **six players** (not five), the shuttle has **8 seats**, and there is a reusable absence rule (`Absence_Protocol.md`). Lines below that say "5 players" or "five players" are out of date.

**Key**
- **KEEP**: carried over as written
- **CHANGE**: same purpose, reworded or retuned for the new premise
- **NEW**: did not exist in the originals
- **CUT**: removed

**New premise in one line:** the players are the same five characters in every act, each with one race/class/subclass from level 3 to 7. Mordax stages four nightmares around them, and his *past victims* (not host bodies) are the people they meet and, in Session 4, the Echoes.
