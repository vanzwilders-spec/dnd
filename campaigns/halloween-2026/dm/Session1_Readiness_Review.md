## 2. Corrections to things I told you earlier [CORRECTION]

1. **"A Wraith is too strong for level 3."** That was too absolute. I've now checked the 2024 stat block (SRD 5.2 data): Wraith is CR 5, 1,800 XP, 67 HP, AC 13, and its Life Drain hits for about 21 necrotic damage **and reduces hit point maximum by the same amount** (until a long rest, as far as I know; the data I pulled didn't spell that part out). For six level-3 characters the XP lands between the Moderate and High budgets (section 4), so it is hard but legal. **The real problem is the damage against d8 characters** (roughly 20 to 28 HP at level 3, my estimate since I can't see the sheets) and a max-HP loss that lasts until a long rest, in a campaign where long rests are rare. I still recommend not using a stock Wraith, but it is a design preference, not a rules necessity.
2. **XP budgets.** The per-character numbers I use (level 3: Low 150, Moderate 225, High 400) come from the old thresholds that the 2024 rules map to Low, Moderate, and High. I have a source for that mapping but not the 2024 table itself, so **treat the budgets as approximate** until checked against the 2024 DMG or 5e.tools.
3. **Source for monsters.** The 5e.tools site doesn't render for me, so I cross-checked 2024 stat blocks against the **SRD 5.2 data on Open5e**. For monsters that appear in the SRD these should match the 2024 Monster Manual. I'll say when something comes from elsewhere.

## 4. Encounter math

Level 3 budget per character, approximate (see correction 2): **Low 150, Moderate 225, High 400.** For six characters: **Low 900 · Moderate 1,350 · High 2,400.** The 2024 rules have no multiplier for number of monsters.

| Encounter | Base (2024 SRD) | CR / XP | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Revenant Hounds (x3)** | Shadow | CR 1/2, 100 XP each, **300 total** | 27 HP, AC 12, Strength Drain |
| **The Collector** | Animated Armor | CR 1, **200 XP** | 33 HP, AC 18, two Slams (1d6+2). No grapple in stock stats. |
| **Harwick, option A** | Specter, scaled up | CR 1 (200 XP) stock; about CR 2 (450 XP) as tuned | 22 HP stock, Life Drain 2d6 plus max-HP loss, flies 50 ft |
| **Harwick, option B** | Ghost, scaled down | CR 4, 1,100 XP stock | 45 HP, two Withering Touch (3d10+3) is too deadly as is |
| **Harwick, option C** | Wraith, stock | CR 5, **1,800 XP** | 67 HP, Life Drain about 21 damage plus max-HP loss |
| **Ashford** | Noble | CR 1/8, 25 XP | 9 HP, AC 15 |
| **Luna** | Commoner (custom HP) | n/a | Not a combat NPC |

**What it means.** With Harwick at option A, the three combat encounters total about 950 XP (300 + 200 + 450), roughly one Low encounter for six characters, spread across the night. For six characters including a barbarian, the Hounds and the Collector will not threaten anyone on HP alone. The danger and the dread have to come from **the clock, the hearths going out, and Harwick**, not from HP. That suits a puzzle-led horror session. **Harwick is the one fight worth real prep.** I recommend option A: scale a Specter up to about CR 2 (more HP, better to-hit) and add the custom "I Must Serve" drag (DC 15 Strength save or grappled and pulled toward the cellar). That keeps him frightening and non-lethal, and a barbarian can hold the chokepoint.
