# Signature items (design notes)

*Working draft. One personal item per player, built from what they already carry. Items may be homebrew by the DM's decision (Oct 12); spells must be real 2024 spells. Nothing here is final until the players answer the questions below.*

## The idea
- Start from the item the character already carries; the item has a story that matters to them.
- If the player has no story, the item *chose them* (a revelation about their character).
- Each item is **an unplanned prop**: something Mordax did not put on his stage. He recoils from it, and it is why the party can fight him at all.
- It need not be a weapon (a cleric's holy symbol or shield is fine).
- Keep power small and similar across the table.

## The template (same for every item)
1. **Magical,** counts as a magic item for overcoming resistance. Always the character's own item.
2. **One 1st-level spell, 1/day,** a real 2024 spell, cast without a slot.
3. **One small flavored trait** (no numbers that outscale the party).
4. **A Mordax tell:** how he reacts when it appears.
5. **Growth:** a small upgrade at Session 3 and a finale moment in Session 4.

## Questions for the players (copy and paste)
One block, same for everyone, with your character's name:

> For your character, tell me: (1) Which thing you carry matters most to them: a weapon, a symbol, a tool? (2) Does it have a story, or where did it come from? (3) If it turned out to be more than it seems, would you prefer it to be a gift, an inheritance, or a surprise that it chose you? (4) Anything it must never become?
