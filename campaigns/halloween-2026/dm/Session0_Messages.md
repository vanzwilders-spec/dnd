# Session 0 messages (Discord)

*Drafts to paste. Send each invitation as a private DM to its player; post the table note in the channel. Nothing here is a spoiler.*

## Table note (post in the channel)

> Hi all. Friday, Oct 16 is Session 1 of our four-session Halloween campaign. A few quick things so we start smoothly:
>
> **Lines and veils.** Tonight's story is horror with a dark sense of humor. Expect ghosts, grief, creepy houses, being separated from the group and being grabbed or pulled. If anything should be off the table or handled off-screen, tell me privately and I will respect it. The X-card is always available: say "X" and we move on.
>
> **Your characters.** Your sheets should be final. If you are still changing something, tell me before Friday. Your characters keep the same race, class and subclass for all four sessions and level up between sessions.
>
> **The dark.** The lights go out early in the first scene. I have Butch and Sassy down as the two with darkvision; tell me if I have yours wrong. Torches, candles and lanterns will matter.
>
> **If you cannot make a session,** tell me as early as you can. Your character steps offstage safely and levels up with the party.
>
> **Optional:** three private lines for me about your character: one thing they fear, one thing they value more than they admit, and one thing they would sacrifice to protect.
>
> **Player Guide:** https://vanzwilders-spec.github.io/dnd/campaigns/halloween-2026/players/Player_Guide.html. It is spoiler-free.
>
> Each of you will get one private message from me with something that arrived for your character. Keep it to yourself until Friday. See you then.
